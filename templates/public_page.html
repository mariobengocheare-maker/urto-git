Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

URTO is a private tool built for and used only by Mario Bengochea, a real estate agent in Miami-Dade County, Florida. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up for or sign in to it.

What URTO stores

URTO stores the real estate client records Mario enters himself: names, phone numbers, email addresses, property addresses, notes, follow-up dates, calendar items, transactions and commission records. This information is kept in URTO's own database and is only accessible after signing in with Mario's password.

How URTO uses Google

With Mario's permission, URTO connects to his own Google account for two purposes:

  • Google Drive (the drive.file permission): URTO saves backup copies of its own data into a single folder in Mario's Google Drive. With this permission URTO can only see and change files it created itself. It cannot read any other files in the Drive.
  • Google Calendar (the calendar.events permission): when Mario schedules a virtual meeting in URTO, URTO creates that one calendar event with a Google Meet link and invites the client email Mario entered.

Information received from Google APIs is used only to provide these two features to Mario. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train any AI or machine-learning model. URTO's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

URTO does not sell or share any personal information. Data leaves URTO only when Mario himself sends it, for example to his own Google Drive backup or to a client he invites to a meeting.

Removing access and data

Google access can be removed at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files in Google Drive can be deleted directly from Drive. Client records can be deleted inside URTO.
